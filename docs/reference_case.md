# Reference case and reproduction of the paper

## Run it

```bash
cmake -S . -B build -G Ninja -DCMAKE_BUILD_TYPE=Release && cmake --build build
./build/dustppd run configs/reference/paper_1m_100au.toml -o ref.csv -s ref.json
```

Python:

```python
import dustppd as dp
res = dp.run(dp.reference_config(), check=True)
print(res["summary"]["t_drift_yr"])
```

All numbers of the paper are regenerated by `python scripts/compare_paper.py`
(writes `docs/validation.md` and `docs/figures/`). Any other case is a CLI override:

```bash
./build/dustppd run configs/reference/paper_1m_100au.toml \
    --set particle.a_init_cm=1.0 --set particle.r_init_au=10 \
    --set physics.coagulation=true --set physics.sedimentation=false
./build/dustppd sweep configs/reference/paper_1m_100au.toml \
    --param particle.a_init_cm --values 1e-4,0.1,1,10,100 --threads 8
```

## Reference case

1 m body, `r_init = 100 au`, `z_init = 3H`, analytic disk, sedimentation + drift.

| quantity | paper | dustppd |
|---|---|---|
| settling time | 252 yr | 251.1 yr |
| drift time | 306 kyr | 304.7 kyr |
| max abs(v_r) | 58.5 m/s at 0.31 au | 54.6 m/s at 0.36 au |
| abs(v_r) at r_in | 33.5 m/s | 29.7 m/s |

## What agrees (within 5 %)

- Table 1: 7 of 8 settling times.
- Table 2 (analytic disk): all drift times of 1 cm, 10 cm and 1 m bodies (8 cases).
- Table 4: all 9 drift times with coagulation (radial-drift-only mode, as `jord++`).
- Fig. 12: final radii of 1 cm (21 cm) and 1 dm (30 cm) grains and all growth times.

## Known discrepancies

1. **Table 2, grains 1 um - 1 cm without coagulation**: our drift times are 10-50 %
   longer (e.g. 1 um from 10 au: 408 kyr vs 344 kyr; 1 mm from 50 au: 1.78 Myr vs
   1.2 Myr). The paper states (Sec. 2.5) that small grains were computed with analytic
   formulas because the numerical code stalled; neither the stationary formula with
   `St` nor Eq. (26) taken literally (with `tau_s`) reproduces these values. For 1 um
   grains the drift is pure gas advection, `t = int dr/|u_r|`, which gives our value
   analytically (tested to 1e-4), so the difference must come from the paper's procedure.
   Notably, the *with-coagulation* values for the same grains (Table 4) agree.
2. **Table 1, 10 cm from 10 au**: 13.2 yr (first entry) vs 9.3 yr.
3. **Fig. 12 final radii of 1 mm and 1 m grains**: 19.7 cm vs 15 cm and 187 cm vs
   125 cm (the 1 cm and 1 dm radii agree).
4. **Table 3 maximum speeds with coagulation** do not agree (ours 131, 141, 174, 49 m/s
   vs 24, 124, 55, 157 m/s). Our 1 dm and 1 m values look like the paper's 1 m and 1 dm
   values swapped; the maximum is reached at the inner edge where the speed is very
   sensitive to the Epstein-Stokes switch (assumption A3).
5. **Peak drift speed of the reference case**: 54.6 vs 58.5 m/s, at 0.36 vs 0.31 au
   (7 % / 15 %), likewise sensitive to the switch position.
6. **GD / MHD rows of Table 2 and Figs. 4, 7** need the "Belmondo" tables, which are not
   available.

The two calibrated parameters (`sigma_mol`, `settle_eps`, see `physics_model.md` A3-A4)
are the main source of uncertainty; both are configurable.
